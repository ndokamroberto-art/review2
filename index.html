Our Story

A Family Passion for English Bulldogs

Welcome to Royal English Bulldogs. We are a small, in-home breeder dedicated to preserving the health, temperament, and iconic charm of the English Bulldog.

Founded in 2014 by the Bennett family, our program focuses on quality over quantity. Every breeding pair is health-tested, and every puppy is raised underfoot with our children and other pets — never in kennels.

  • AKC Registered: All puppies come with champion lineage and full registration papers.
  • Health Certified: OFA certified hips, elbows, and trachea on all parent dogs.
  • Vet Ready: Puppies come vet-checked, vaccinated, dewormed, and microchipped.
  • Worldwide Delivery: We ship safely to most countries with a licensed pet nanny.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your English Bulldog puppies cost?

Our puppies range from $4,200 to $6,800 depending on color, markings, and lineage. Rare colors like lilac merle and chocolate tri are at the higher end. All prices include AKC registration, first vaccinations, microchip, and a go-home kit.

Do you offer worldwide shipping or delivery?

Yes! We offer personal delivery, flight nanny service to most major international airports, and climate-controlled ground transport. We'll work with you to find the safest, most affordable option for your location.

What health testing do you do?

All our breeding dogs are OFA certified for hips, elbows, and trachea. We also run DNA panels for breed-specific conditions like degenerative myelopathy, CMR, and HUU. Puppies come with a 2-year written genetic health guarantee.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Click the "Reserve" button on any puppy card, fill out the contact form, and we'll reach out within 24 hours to schedule your reservation with the provided details. A 50% deposit secures your puppy, with the balance due before pickup or delivery.

Are English Bulldogs good with children and other pets?

Absolutely. When properly socialized from a young age, English Bulldogs are affectionate, gentle, and wonderful with children and other pets. Our puppies are raised in our home with kids and cats, so they're well-adjusted from day one.

What should I expect regarding grooming and exercise?

English Bulldogs have short coats that require minimal grooming — a weekly brush and occasional bath is enough. They need moderate daily exercise (2-3 short walks) and love a good nap. Be mindful of overheating in hot weather due to their brachycephalic nature.
